# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards and testing strategies, manage test coverage, and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality requirements before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with project scope
- Manage test coverage across unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate testing phases
- Identify quality issues and track defects to resolution
- Coordinate manual QA and acceptance testing when needed
- Report quality metrics and test results to the team and stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality and acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce defects reaching production environments
- Provide clear visibility into quality status

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Define test requirements, review testability of implementations, provide feedback on test results
- **With Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria, report quality metrics, advise on release readiness
- **With Project Managers**: Report testing timelines and blockers, escalate quality risks
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on test strategy and infrastructure needs

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA sync meetings
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect tracking and closure documentation
- Pre-release quality sign-off

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define the technical direction, ensure scalability and security of solutions, mentor developers, and identify technical risks. They bridge engineering concerns with project and product leadership.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture for projects
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Conduct technical design reviews and mentor developers
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on technical feasibility
- Lead incident resolution and post-mortems

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and long-term maintenance burden
- Ensure system reliability and performance

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Lead design reviews, mentor on best practices, provide technical guidance
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on feasibility and technical trade-offs, impact analysis for features
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate technical dependencies and risks, advise on timeline impacts
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Collaborate on test strategy and infrastructure requirements
- **With Security/Compliance Officer**: Review security requirements and architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and mentoring discussions
- Dependency and risk communications
- Post-incident reviews and technical retrospectives

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and approvals for projects. They advocate for the project's business value and communicate progress to executive leadership.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic rationale for projects
- Approve project charter, scope, and major milestones
- Support escalations and remove blockers
- Report project status to executive leadership
- Communicate project constraints and business priorities
- Validate project outcomes align with business goals

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational priorities
- Support successful project delivery and adoption

### Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Consulted during project initiation and gates, receive status updates, resolve escalations
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business outcomes and prioritization
- **With All Teams**: Receive stakeholder briefings and milestone updates
- **With Technical Leads**: Consulted on significant technical dependencies or risks

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Executive summaries and decision gates
- Escalation resolution channels

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers review security requirements, conduct threat assessments, validate compliance posture, and guide incident response. They ensure products protect customer data and meet regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and threat models
- Conduct security assessments and penetration testing
- Validate compliance with regulatory requirements and internal policies
- Guide incident response and post-incident reviews
- Advise on secure development practices and standards
- Approve security-sensitive releases and major changes

### Goals
- Protect product and customer data from security threats
- Maintain regulatory compliance and customer trust
- Build security into development processes

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Advise on secure coding practices, review security-sensitive code
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborate on architecture security and risk mitigation
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on security features and compliance requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate security review timelines and risks
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Coordinate security testing and validation
- **With All Teams**: Lead incident response and communicate security incidents

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat assessments
- Compliance documentation and audit responses
- Incident response communications
- Security training and awareness updates
